# Apius Pilot — Purpose

> A single **private, controlled pilot** of ScaleSignals with Apius Pharmacy and
> Healthcare Services. Pilot evidence is **pilot-scoped**: it is not a product
> dataset and never flows to Vantage.

## Why this pilot exists

To produce the first credible proof of the ScaleSignals promise —
*"ScaleSignals runs the work around your raise, while you run your company"* —
on real company context, under controlled conditions, before any scaling.

## First-value definition (the bar)

The pilot delivers first-value when ScaleSignals produces:

1. one credible company source;
2. an evidence-grounded interpretation of the company;
3. three credible South African / Africa-relevant healthcare capital providers
   or pathways;
4. transparent evidence and explicit uncertainty;
5. at least one safe, serious next action;
6. **no provider contact**;
7. **no unsupported fit claim**.

## Conditions and constraints

- **No provider contact.** Analysis only.
- **No unsupported fit claims.** Any fit must be evidence-backed under
  `provider-intelligence/evidence-rules.yaml`.
- **Conservative status.** Validation is **not yet complete**; nothing here
  asserts that first-value has been achieved.
- **Boundary.** Apius/company-private material stays within ScaleSignals; it
  does not become Vantage intelligence.

## Records in this pilot

- `company-evidence.yaml` — accepted, pilot-scoped company evidence.
- `provider-candidates.yaml` — candidate providers/pathways under evidence rules
  (candidates only; no endorsement).
- `missing-evidence.yaml` — known gaps that cap confidence.
- `outcome-log.yaml` — what was delivered and how it scored against first-value.
