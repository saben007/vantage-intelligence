# ScaleSignals — Current Priority (conservative mirror)

> This is a **conservative description** maintained by the Learning Spine. It is
> not the source of truth for the running product, and editing it never changes
> the product. Founder-private material does not belong here.

## Audience

Founders raising capital.

## Governing promise

> *"ScaleSignals runs the work around your raise, while you run your company."*

## Current priority

Prove first-value in the **private controlled Apius pilot** (see
`pilots/apius/`). The priority is **credible, honest, useful output** — not lead
volume and not introductions.

First-value is delivered when ScaleSignals produces, on real company context:

1. one credible company source;
2. an evidence-grounded interpretation of the company;
3. three credible South African / Africa-relevant healthcare capital providers
   or pathways;
4. transparent evidence and explicit uncertainty;
5. at least one safe, serious next action;
6. **no provider contact**;
7. **no unsupported fit claim**.

## What is explicitly out of scope right now

- Contacting any provider.
- Claiming a fit that the evidence does not support.
- Scaling beyond the controlled pilot before first-value is validated.
- Any flow of pilot or founder-private data toward Vantage.
