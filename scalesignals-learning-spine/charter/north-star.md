# North Star

The Learning Spine serves two products with **separate** purposes and
**separate** audiences. They share a quality philosophy — source-grounded,
explainable, honest about uncertainty — but they must never share private data.

---

## Founder-side purpose — ScaleSignals

**Audience:** founders raising capital.

**Governing promise:**

> *"ScaleSignals runs the work around your raise, while you run your company."*

ScaleSignals earns trust by doing the unglamorous coordination work of a raise
*for* the founder, so the founder's attention stays on building the company.

The immediate proof is a **private, controlled pilot** with Apius Pharmacy and
Healthcare Services. First-value for the founder is defined as delivering, on
real company context:

- one credible company source;
- an evidence-grounded interpretation of that company;
- three credible South African / Africa-relevant healthcare capital providers
  or pathways;
- transparent evidence and explicit uncertainty;
- at least one safe, serious next action;
- **no provider contact**;
- **no unsupported fit claim**.

The founder-side win is **trust through honesty and useful next actions**, not
volume of leads and not premature introductions.

## Institutional-side purpose — Vantage

**Audience:** GPs, LPs, DFIs, family offices, allocators, strategic investment
teams.

**Aim:** support better capital-deployment decisions using intelligence that is
**source-grounded, permissioned and explainable**.

Vantage earns trust by being defensible: every claim traceable to a source,
every permission respected, every limitation stated. It is an institutional
decision-support layer, not a founder tool and not a marketplace.

## Why they are kept apart

The two purposes create different obligations:

- ScaleSignals holds **founder-private** information given in confidence during
  a raise.
- Vantage produces **investor-facing** intelligence.

Letting founder-private material silently become investor-side intelligence
would break the founder's trust and the core promise above. Therefore the two
remain architecturally and operationally separate until — and unless — a future
consent, privacy and governance architecture is **separately approved**
(see `product-boundaries.md` and `data-and-consent-policy.md`).

## What "good" looks like for the Learning Spine itself

- Decisions are preserved with their reasoning and can be audited later.
- Current state is always conservative and explicit — never optimistic by
  default.
- Contradictions between stated purpose and observed outcomes are surfaced, not
  hidden.
- Ben's coordination burden falls over time **without** his authority being
  removed or his boundaries being crossed.
